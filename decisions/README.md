# Decisions

Store durable architecture, workflow, and strategy decisions here when the rationale matters for future work.

Recommended lightweight ADR format:

```md
# ADR-NNN: Decision title

## Context
What problem/constraint caused this decision?

## Decision
What was chosen?

## Alternatives
What realistic alternatives were considered?

## Consequences
What improves, what becomes harder, and what must be revisited?

## Evidence
Links to experiments, benchmarks, incidents, or source material.

## Status
proposed | accepted | superseded
```

Do not use ADRs for trivial implementation details.
